Is the Earth round or flat?

Short answer: it’s a globe, slightly squashed at the poles. Here’s the evidence anyone can check.

See the proof ↓
1

Ships sink below the horizon

A ship sailing away vanishes hull first, then the mast. On a flat surface it would only get smaller, never disappear from the bottom up.

Curvature
2

Earth’s shadow is always round

During every lunar eclipse, no matter the time or angle, the shadow on the Moon has a curved edge. Only a sphere does that every time. A flat disc would sometimes give a thin line or an oval.

Eclipses
3

Different places, different stars

From India you can see the North Star. From Australia you can’t, but you see the Southern Cross. If Earth were flat, everyone would see the same sky.

Astronomy
4

Day here, night there

When it’s noon in one country, it’s midnight in another. A single Sun lights only half of a rotating globe, which neatly explains time zones. A flat Earth can’t do this.

Time zones
5

Flights fly curved shortcuts

The shortest route between far-apart cities is a “great circle”. On a flat map it looks bent, but on a globe it’s straight. Routes in the southern hemisphere, like Sydney to Johannesburg, only make sense on a globe.

Aviation
6

2,000-year-old measurement

Around 240 BCE, Eratosthenes saw that the Sun cast no shadow in Syene, while a stick in Alexandria did. The difference in angle revealed a curved surface, and he estimated Earth’s size surprisingly well.

History

More evidence (7 to 12)

🏙️

7. Height changes the view

The higher you stand, the farther you see. From a tall tower you can watch the sunset again after it set at ground level. Eye height of 1.7 m sees a horizon about 4.7 km away.

⛵

8. Sailing around the world

The Magellan–Elcano expedition (1519–1522) sailed west and came back from the east. Pilots and sailors do this today in either direction.

🌀

9. Foucault pendulum & cyclones

A long pendulum slowly changes its swing direction as Earth turns beneath it. Cyclones spin opposite ways in the two hemispheres (Coriolis effect).

🌞

10. Seasons & midnight sun

Near the poles the Sun stays up for months in summer. This happens because a sphere is tilted about 23.4° as it orbits the Sun.

🪐

11. Gravity makes spheres

Gravity pulls mass toward its center, so large bodies like the Moon, Mars and Jupiter are round. Telescopes show them as globes, not discs.

📏

12. Precise measurements

Surveying and satellites show Earth is slightly wider at the equator (about 12,756 km) than pole to pole (about 12,714 km): an oblate spheroid.

And from space…

🛰️

Satellites

Thousands orbit Earth, and GPS only works because it accounts for Earth’s shape.

👩‍🚀

Astronauts

Crews from many countries, including India, the USA, Russia and China, report the same round Earth.

📸

Photos

Independent space agencies and private companies all see the same globe.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Earth flat or round?

Round. Earth is a globe, more exactly an oblate spheroid: nearly a sphere, slightly wider at the equator than pole to pole.

What is the proof that the Earth is round?

Ships vanish hull first, the Moon’s eclipse shadow is always round, different stars show in each hemisphere, time zones give day and night at once, flights follow great-circle routes, and space photos and measurements show a globe.

Why do ships disappear hull first?

The surface curves, so the lower part of a distant ship is hidden behind the curve first and the mast stays visible longest.

What is the shape of the Earth called?

An oblate spheroid: slightly flattened at the poles and bulging at the equator because Earth rotates.

Who first measured the size of the Earth?

Eratosthenes, around 240 BCE, by comparing Sun shadow angles in two cities.

Why do some people believe it is flat?

Earth is so big that its curve is hard to notice from the ground, and distrust and online echo chambers reinforce the idea.

Key terms

Oblate spheroidA sphere flattened at the poles and bulging at the equator.
Great circleThe largest circle on a sphere. It is the shortest route between two points.
HorizonThe line where the surface seems to meet the sky; it drops farther away as height increases.
CurvatureHow much a surface bends. Earth’s is tiny over short distances.
Coriolis effectApparent deflection of moving air and water caused by Earth’s rotation.
Axial tiltEarth’s axis is tilted about 23.4°, which causes seasons.

Self-test (click a question to see the answer)

1. What disappears first as a ship sails away?

The hull (bottom) goes first, then the mast, because the surface curves.

2. What shape is Earth’s shadow on the Moon in a lunar eclipse?

Always round. Only a sphere casts a round shadow from every angle.

3. Which star can you see only from the northern hemisphere?

The North Star (Polaris).

4. Who measured Earth’s size using shadows, and when?

Eratosthenes, around 240 BCE.

5. Why is Earth not a perfect sphere?

Its rotation makes it bulge at the equator, so it is an oblate spheroid.
